# Paper figure captions and provenance

All figures are generated by `generate_paper_figures.py`. They use the verified experiment completed on 2026-10-14. No model was retrained for figure generation. Every figure is supplied as vector PDF, 600-dpi PNG, and 600-dpi LZW-compressed TIFF.

1. **Model comparison on the held-out test period.** RMSE, MAE, and R² for persistence, LSTM, CNN-LSTM, Conv3D encoder-decoder, and Transformer on 358 aligned 2025 targets (2025-01-08 to 2025-12-31). Checkpoints for learned models were selected by validation loss. The R² panel uses a truncated vertical range (0.965–0.990) to show small differences; interpret it with the numeric labels.
2. **Input-window ablation.** Conv3D test RMSE and MAE using 3, 7, and 14 past daily maps, compared with persistence on the same 351 dates (2025-01-15 to 2025-12-31). Each learned checkpoint was selected using its validation loss. Differences are not significance-tested.
3. **Seasonal test errors.** RMSE and MAE by season for the selected Conv3D model, based on its 358 aligned 2025 targets. Seasonal sample counts are shown under each category.
4. **Spatial distribution of test errors.** Gridpoint RMSE and MAE calculated directly from saved selected-checkpoint predictions and targets across all 358 test dates. The panel plots the regular 121 × 141 grid across 5°–35°N, 65°–100°E and does not overlay political boundaries.
5. **Representative test prediction.** Previous-day maximum temperature, observed next-day maximum, Conv3D prediction, and absolute error for the test target whose spatial RMSE is the median among the test dates. Color ranges are shared for the three temperature maps. The selected date is printed in the figure title.
6. **Training/validation curves.** Recorded validation loss by epoch for Conv3D, CNN-LSTM, Transformer, and the repaired LSTM run. Markers indicate the validation-selected checkpoint epoch. LSTM history is read from `experiments/reviewer_lstm_repaired_20261014/` because its earlier run log stopped after six epochs.

The Conv3D prediction figures are produced by loading `experiments/reviewer_final_20261014/convlstm_best.pth` and `data/processed/tensors/test_X.npy` / `test_y.npy`, then applying the saved training-only Celsius normalization statistics in `data/processed/mean_std.json`. Test reanalysis targets remain an ERA5-based reference, not independent station observations.
